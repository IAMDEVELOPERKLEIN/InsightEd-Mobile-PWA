InsightEd Operational Guide

Empowering School Leadership through Data-Driven Insights

01

Quick Start & Installation

InsightEd is a **Progressive Web App (PWA)** designed to operate directly from your mobile browser while remaining "offline-ready."

1.1 PWA Installation

Open Chrome (Android) or Safari (iOS) and navigate to tinyurl.com/InsightEdV2.

1.2 Add to Home Screen

Select "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App" from your browser menu.

1.3 Launch

Open the InsightEd icon from your home screen to begin.

02

The Nexus Gateway

Upon launching, navigate to the correct entry point to unlock your administrative nodes.

Step Alpha

Select the "InsightED (For HROD)" path.

Step Beta

Locate and select the "School Head Portal".

Click "Create Account" for Digital Identity.

03

Registration & Identity

3.3 IERN Registry

Your unique **IERN (Institutional Entity Reference Number)** is your primary digital key. Record this immediately—it is required for all future syncs.

3.1 Personal Mapping

First/Last Name, official Email, and Contact Number are mandatory fields.

3.2 Hierarchy Path

Region → Division → District → School Level → School ID.

3.4 Precision Mapping: Drag the pin to the school's geographical center.

5

Unit 1: School Profile & Identity

5.1 Basic Info: DO NOT use abbreviations for the School Name. Verify all location details.

5.2 Curricular Offering: Select specific authorized offering (e.g., K-6, K-12).

5.3 School Head Info: Name autofilled. Input Middle Name, Sex, Position, DOB, Date Appointed.

5.4 Ownership: Select Month/Year established and Ownership Type.

5.5 Documentation: Upload supporting document (Deed of Donation, etc.) in **PDF format** only.

5.6 Annex/Extension: Input Extension School ID or Mother School ID accordingly.

06

Unit 2: Modular Enrollment

6.2 The Mandatory Disable Gate

You MUST either input enrollment or click "Disable" for every grade level. This dynamic filter hides inactive grades across all subsequent units to streamline your workflow.

6.5 Gender Validation

The "Magic Math" validation automatically triggers once Total Male and Total Female counts are encoded. Discrepancies will block unit finalization.

6.4 Special Populations

Ensure precise reporting for SNEd (Special Needs) and ARAL program participants.

07

Unit 3: Section Standards

7.2 Standards Breakdown

Distribute your total sections into three specific categories: Less than Standard, Within Standard, and Above Standard.

7.3 Mathematical Integrity

The sum of these three categories must **EXACTLY match** the Total Sections for that grade level.

Algorithm Guard
LT + WS + AS
= TOTAL SECTIONS
08

Unit 4: Specialized Profiles

8.1 Community Toggles

Toggle ALS, Muslim, IP, Displaced, or Overage groups. Only selected groups will activate input fields to reduce data clutter.

8.3 Nutritional Matrix

Input counts for Severely Wasted, Wasted, and Overweight/Obese.

Normal status: SYSTEM CALCULATED

8.2 Attrition Data

If repeaters or dropouts are present, select "Yes" to unlock grade-level specific reporting.

09

Unit 5: Modality & Shifting

9.1 100% In-Person Setup

Answer "Yes" only if the school operates on a **100% In-Person, Single Shift** model.

9.2 Mixed Shifting Models

Configure Double or Triple shifts and specialized delivery modes (Blended/Distance) per active grade.

9.3 Emergency ADM Node

Identify Alternative Delivery Modes (Modular, Online) activated due to local disasters or extreme congestion.

10

Unit 7: Infrastructure Audit

10.1 Seating Audit Node

Input functional and broken seats. Critical shortages require the manual verification keyword:

"CONFIRM"

10.3 WASH & Utilities

Report water sources and toilet counts. Off-grid power status also requires explicit confirmation.

10.2 ICT & eCarts Inventory

Catalog all digital assets. For eCarts, specify Batch Name, Funding Year, and official Source.

11

Unit 8: Building Register

11.1 Define spaces with precise length/width mapping.

11.2 Log Building Name, Storeys, and Condition status.

11.3 Assign Room functionality (Instructional vs Non-Instructional).

11.4 Repair Protocol

Use the 0-100% damage slider to trigger repair action items.

12

Unit 9: Environment Profile

12.1 Terrain & Accessibility

Record transportation modes, road passability, and proximity to environmental hazards.

12.3 Proximity Matrix

Quantify distance (km) and travel time (mins) to Hospital, Brgy Hall, and SDO.

12.4 Holistic Risk Score

System generates a 0–10 Risk Profile based on environmental inputs. Finalization triggers the Division sync.
