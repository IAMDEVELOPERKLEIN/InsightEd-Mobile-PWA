Installation & Landing

1.1 PWA Installation

InsightEd is a **Progressive Web App (PWA)**. It operates directly from your mobile browser for offline-readiness.

  • Open Chrome (Android) or Safari (iOS).
  • Navigate to: tinyurl.com/InsightEdV2
  • Select "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App" from your browser menu.

Technical Protocol: Offline-First

Unlike traditional apps, InsightEd's PWA architecture allows you to continue monitoring even in low-signal areas. Any data entered while offline is safely cached until you perform a manual sync.

1.2 Choosing Your Portal (The Nexus)

Upon launching the app, you will arrive at the **InsightED Nexus**. You must select the correct path to access your tools:

  1. Select the "InsightED (For HROD)" path.
  2. Locate and select the "School Head Portal" from the options.

5

Data Health Scoring Logic

Protocol Deductions (-25 pts each)
Enrollment reported without corresponding teachers, toilets, or classrooms.
Unrealistic Pupil-to-Teacher ratios (<15 or >65 students per class).
Zero counts on critical facilities in active schools.
6

Offline Sync Protocol

The app saves inputs locally. Navigate to the **Sync Dashboard** once a stable connection is established to transmit data.

file_name: manual_data_sync_success.gif

DO NOT LOG OUT

Logging out while sync is pending will result in permanent data loss.
