07
Scale & Density

Unit 3: Sectioning

"Analyze classroom density by mapping sections against national standards."

Validation Rule

"LT + WS + AS Protocol"

Categorize sections into LT (Below), WS (Within), and AS (Above) standards. The sum must exactly match the total sections reported per grade.

Unit 3 Sectioning Guard Analytics

Grade-Level Audit: Total teachers must be distributed according to actual load.

Standard Density: WS (Within Standard) is the target operational node for all grade levels.
