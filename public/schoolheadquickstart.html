Quick Start

Installation Guide

At a Glance

  • Install InsightEd as an app on your phone.
  • Works just like a regular app from the Play Store/App Store.

"InsightEd is a **Phone-First** app for school reporting."

1. Open Chrome/Safari.

2. Go to: tinyurl.com/InsightEdV2.

3. Tap **Menu** → **Add to Home Screen**.

05
Your School Identity

Unit 1: School Profile

At a Glance

  • Set School Name, ID, and Curricular Offering.
  • Upload Ownership Documents (DepEd/LGU/Private).
  • Establish establishment date and Mother/Annex links.
To change the School ID, click "Unlock" and type "CONFIRM".

Essential Data

  • • Enter the official School Name (no abbreviations).
  • • Select your authorized Curricular Offering (e.g., K-6, K-12).
  • • Enter the official Month and Year of establishment.

Ownership & Links

Select ownership type and upload the deed/document. If applicable, provide Mother or Annex School IDs.

06
Learner Count

Unit 2: Learners

At a Glance

  • Enter total enrollment per grade level.
  • Mandatory: Split counts by Male and Female.
  • Disable grades not present in your school.

Enrollment Modes

  • A. Monograde: Enter totals for each grade.
  • B. Multigrade: Use "ADD COMBO" for grade pairings and enter specific counts.
  • C. Mixed: Combine multigrade combos with regular grades.
The system blocks sync if Male + Female does not equal the Total Enrollment.
07
Classroom Density

Unit 3: Sectioning

At a Glance

  • Create sections and assign shifts (Morning/Afternoon/Whole Day).
  • Categorize sections by enrollment density (LT, WS, AS).
  • Ensure section totals match your Unit 2 Enrollment.

Density Categories

LT (Less Than): Below standard ratio.

WS (Within Standard): Optimal learner-teacher ratio.

AS (Above Standard): Overcrowded sections.

The sum of LT + WS + AS MUST equal the total sections declared for that grade level.
08
Special Education & Health

Unit 4: Profiles

At a Glance

  • Select specific learner communities (IP, Muslim, ALS).
  • Record health metrics (Wasted, Severely Wasted).
  • Track performance data (Dropouts, Repeaters).

Community Filters

Select all that apply: ALS, Muslim Affairs, IP Learners, Displaced, Overage.

"Only selected categories will appear for data entry."

Health & Performance

Input counts for nutritional status (Wasted) and learner attrition (Dropouts).

09
Classroom Logic

Unit 5: Learning Modalities

At a Glance

  • Select delivery modes (Face-to-Face, Blended, Distance).
  • Set Shifting Models (Single, Double, or Triple shifts).
  • Report any Emergency ADM (Modular, Online, TV/Radio).

Delivery Ratios

  • 100% In-Person: Full Face-to-Face.
  • Blended (3:2 or 4:1): Mixed on-campus and remote.
  • Full Distance: 0% on-campus.
Selecting "Standard Set-up" auto-fills default F2F values.
10
School Property

Unit 6: School Resources

At a Glance

  • Inventory desks, chairs, and learning materials.
  • Report health clinic and WASH facility details.
  • Record internet and power source connectivity.

1. Furniture & ICT

Audit seat counts (Armchairs, Tables) and ICT assets (Laptops, TVs, eCarts).

2. Utilities & WASH

Log Power source, Internet type (Fiber/Starlink), and Toilet seat counts.

11
Building Audit

Unit 7: Physical Facilities

At a Glance

  • Audit every building and room in the school.
  • Assess repair needs and classroom conditions.
  • Map "Buildable Spaces" for future expansion.

1. Campus Map

Place pins on "Buildable Areas" and input dimensions (m) for total area calculation.

2. Building & Room Audit

Register buildings, set floors, and assign room types. Use the Damage Slider for repair assessments.

12
School Environment

Unit 8: Terrain & Risk Profile

At a Glance

  • Identify flood, earthquake, and landslide risks.
  • Note if the school is used as an evacuation center.
  • List proximity to SDO and Health facilities.

1. Location Hazards

Describe terrain (Level, Hilly) and physical threats (Near Cliff, River Crossings).

2. Risk Exposure

Report recent natural calamities (Floods, Typhoons) and man-made threats.

DO NOT LOG OUT

Logging out may cause sync issues. Please keep the app open or minimized.
