Unit 2: Learners

Enrollment & Grade Classification Protocol

"Dynamic node activation based on your Unit 1 Curricular Offering."

Phase 01 / Node Configuration

Grade levels are displayed according to your Unit 1 selections. CRITICAL ACTION: DISABLE MISSING GRADES You MUST click "Disable" for any grade level your school does not have. These grades will then be hidden in all future audit units.

A. Monograde Setup

Standard input: Enter total enrollment, then verify exact **Male and Female** counts for every grade level.

Standard Protocol

B. Purely Multigrade

  • Step 01: Click "ADD COMBO" for every multigrade pairing in your school.
  • Step 02: Input individual internal enrollment by grade level for the selected combo.
  • Step 03: You MUST click "Disable" for all other grade levels not included in any combo to proceed.
Combination Matrix

C. Mixed Model

Perform **"ADD COMBO"** for multigrade sections first. Then proceed to input enrollment for all other active regular grade levels.

Hybrid Protocol

Node Integrity Rule: Magic Math

"Every input node for total enrollment triggers a mandatory Gender Audit."

You MUST input Male and Female counts for every active grade level. The system blocks final sync if the summation is mismatched.

Scale & Density

Unit 3: Sectioning

Sectioning & Capacity Logic

"Mapping classroom density against national operational standards."

Node Integrity: Unit 2 Connection

This unit is programmatically LOCKED to your Unit 2 Enrollment. You cannot input section counts or distributions that mathematically contradict the total learner nodes reported in the previous phase.

Category 01

Less Than Standard (LT)

Sections with enrollment density significantly below the required classroom ratio.

Target
Category 02

Within Standard (WS)

Sections that match the optimal learner-to-teacher density for efficient instruction.

Category 03

Above Standard (AS)

Overcrowded sections with density exceeding the national classroom capacity norms.

Operational Audit Sequence

  • Input total sections per active grade level.
  • Distribute sections into LT, WS, and AS buckets.

"The summation of LT + WS + AS MUST exactly equal the total sections declared for that grade node. Mismatched counts will block the Unit 3 synchronization arrow."

Unit 7: Physical Facilities & Architecture Audit

"This unit uses a 4-Step Wizard to build a digital twin of your school’s physical infrastructure."

Step 1: Visual Multi-Space Builder 🏗️

Define the "buildable footprint" of your campus using an interactive map.

Locating

Place a pin on the center of a buildable area.

Dimensions

Input Length and Width in meters.

Computed Area

Automatically calculates Total Area (sqm).

Purpose

Establishes building placement or existing structure nodes.

Step 2: Register Buildings 🏢

Log every major structure on your campus with vertical and capacity data.

  • A
    Building Profile

    Name (no abbreviations), Category (Academic, Multi-Purpose), and Year.

  • B
    Verticality & Capacity

    Define Storeys (floors) and total Classroom count.

  • C
    Condition Nodes

    Good, Repairs, or **Condemned** (Requires Age/Safety/Damage reason).

Step 3: Granular Room Setup 🏫

Refine the details for every functional space within your buildings.

CRITICAL PROTOCOL

"You must assign a grade level (Monograde, Multigrade, or Non-Instructional) to all rooms before proceeding."

Room Identity

Customize Name & verify dimensions.

Inventory

Input current Seat capacity node.

Step 4: Repair Assessment 🛠️

Active only for rooms marked as "Needs Repair." A detailed damage audit is required.

Validation Guard
A **Damage Checklist:** Roofing, Ceiling, Windows, etc.
B **Damage Ratio:** Slider input from 0% to 100% severity.
C **Action:** Select Routine, Major Rehab, or Demolition.

"Audit submission is blocked if repair assessments are incomplete."
