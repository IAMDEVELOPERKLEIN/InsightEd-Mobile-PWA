EFD Project Monitoring Suite

EFD Engineers act as administrators for the field engineering staff.

Action: Tap the [Assign] button on any project card to open the Division Engineer directory. Select the staff responsible for field monitoring of that site.

Tap the [Logs] icon to view a chronological history of status changes, including reasons for suspension or termination provided by field engineers.

06

Mother MOA & Resolutions Archive

The EFD Engineer's primary planning task is managing the national archive of LGU Memorandums.

6.1 UPLOAD PROTOCOLS

  • • LGU Type: PGO (Province), CGO (City), or MGO (Municipality). *PGO not applicable for NCR regions.
  • • Document Pair: Official Archiving requires uploading both the **Mother MOA** and the **Sangguniang Resolution**.

6.2 SUPPLEMENTAL MOA

For projects requiring contract extensions or variations, add **Supplemental MOAs** to the established archive. You must link these to a valid **IPC ID** for project-wide visibility.

Archive Explorer

All Mother MOA records are searchable by LGU Name or Region. Documents are viewable via the built-in PDF explorer with zero external downloads required.
