01

App Installation (PWA)

InsightEd is a Progressive Web App (PWA). It operates directly from your mobile browser for maximum performance during on-site inspections.

1.1 iOS ACCESS (SAFARI)

  1. 1. Open tinyurl.com/InsightEdV2
  2. 2. Tap the "Share" icon (square with arrow up)
  3. 3. Scroll down and select "Add to Home Screen"
  4. 4. Tap Add in the top right

1.2 ANDROID ACCESS (CHROME)

  1. 1. Open tinyurl.com/InsightEdV2
  2. 2. Tap the "Three Dots" menu button
  3. 3. Select "Install App" or "Add to Home Screen"
  4. 4. Confirm by tapping Install

SOP MANDATORY RULE

NEVER CLEAR BROWSER CACHE. InsightEd stores your on-site evidence in the local PWA buffer. Clearing cache will result in PERMANENT DATA LOSS of unsynced logs.

06

Construction Update Protocol

SOP Mandatory Protocol

Division Engineers must follow the 3-Step Wizard for all site progress reporting. Accomplishment reports WITHOUT categorized photographic evidence are non-compliant.

Step 1: Media Evidence Guidelines

🏗️ Internal Photos

  • • Classrooms: Focus on longest wall, lighting, and outlets.
  • • Height: Capture at 1.4m – 1.6m eye-level.
  • • Example: A shot of the installed electrical panel and ceiling fans.

🌳 External Photos

  • • Cardinal Angles: Front, Left, Right, and Rear wide shots.
  • • Ortho Views: Aerial/Wide shots at 20-30m height (if possible).
  • • Example: A full façade shot showing completed painting and windows.

Step 2: Status & Accomplishment Slider

Percentage Logic: Adjust the slider to match site measurement.

0% (Not Started) 1% - 99% (Ongoing) 100% (Completed / For Final Inspection)

💡 Protocol Note: "For Final Inspection" and "Completed" statuses automatically lock the slider at 100%.

Status As Of Date: Crucial for time-series analytics. Always set to the actual date of visit, not the date of data entry.

09

Variation Orders (VO) Management

VARIATION ORDER #1
SUBMIT VO

Budget/Timeline Realignment

What it is: Legal changes to the project's Contract Amount or Target Completion Date due to site conditions.

How to use: Tap the ⚖️ VO button on the project card. Enter the Net Variation amount (+/-) and the Time Extension (days).

Example: Weather-related delays exceeding 10 days require a Time Extension VO for timeline health compliance.
